What is Navigator?

Creativity without the technical barrier.

TAIT Navigator Automation Platform™ is a scalable system of software, hardware and infrastructure supporting show control and machinery automation. Motion-control tools and rules-based conditional cues allow shot designers and programmers to make changes quickly and efficiently while maintaining a safety-led workflow.

Built from industrial robotics and networking technologies, the system turns complex motion into programmable, repeatable movement.

About us

Specialist rigging for film & television.

Winches & Wirecams is a highly specialised rigging company with more than 30 years of experience in the film and television industry. Its credits include work across productions for DC, Marvel, Disney, Netflix and Warner Bros.

The team has worked alongside renowned stunt coordinators and rigging experts, including as part of the award-winning team recognised with the Best Rigging award at the Taurus Awards for work on The Batman.
